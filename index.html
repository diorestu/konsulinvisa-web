KITAS vs. KITAP: Which One Do You Need?

Many foreign nationals confuse the Limited Stay Permit (KITAS) with the Permanent Stay Permit (KITAP). Here is the straightforward comparison:

LIMITED STAY (1-2 YEARS)

KITAS / ITAS

Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas

The standard temporary residence permit for foreigners working, investing, retiring, or living with an Indonesian spouse.

  • ✓ Duration: 1 Year to 2 Years per issuance.
  • ✓ Multiple Entry: Includes MERP (enter and exit Indonesia freely).
  • ✓ Local Privileges: Open Indonesian bank accounts, buy cars/motorbikes, apply for Indonesian driver’s license (SIM).
  • ✓ Sponsorship: Requires Indonesian PT, PT PMA, or Indonesian spouse sponsor.
  • ✓ Pathway: Can be converted to KITAP after qualifying period.
Consult KITAS Application →
PERMANENT RESIDENCY (5 YEARS)

KITAP / ITAP

Kartu Izin Tinggal Tetap

The highest residency tier granted by Indonesia. Zero annual renewal hassle, maximum legal stability, and official KTP-WNA identity card.

  • ★ Duration: 5 Years with automatic lifetime renewal rights.
  • ★ Permanent MERP: 2-Year multiple entry re-entry permits indefinitely.
  • ★ Full Rights: Get Indonesian National ID (KTP WNA), Family Card (KK), local bank credit, and property leasehold rights.
  • ★ Work Rights (Spouses): Foreign spouses on KITAP can work informally or run family businesses without work permits.
  • ★ Eligibility: Foreign spouse married to WNI for 2+ consecutive years on KITAS, or long-term investors/directors.
